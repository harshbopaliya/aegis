Execution Governance Model

Each outbound operation generated by an agent is normalized into a structured AgentAction payload.

The payload is validated through registry/token controls, checked by policy, simulated, risk scored, optionally escalated for human decision, and then executed with backup and audit controls.

Defense in depth: A single blocking decision halts the pipeline immediately, rolls back staged effects, and records an immutable audit event.

Core Platform Capabilities

Identity and Scope Controls

Registry lookup and token scope evaluation validate caller identity and enforce role boundaries.

Sandbox Simulation

Risky operations are replayed in isolated simulation contexts to predict impact before production execution.

Risk and Approval Routing

Risk scoring and policy outcomes determine when actions proceed directly or require human approval.

Execution and Audit Safety

Write paths can snapshot before execution, and every decision is persisted to the observability stream.
